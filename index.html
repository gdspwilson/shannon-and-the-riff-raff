Band Bio

S&TRR deliver an immersive live experience at the intersection of unfiltered rock, raw funk, and soulful blues.

Driven by powerhouse vocals, a tight rhythm section, and inventive guitar jams, we go beyond routine covers. Pairing timeless classics with modern deep cuts, S&TRR elevate every track to its peak performance— delivering a captivating show curated for true live music fans.

Not a tribute band. Not a party band. Not an average cover band.
